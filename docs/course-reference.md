## Proposed continuation, not yet implemented

Use short sublessons and cumulative checkpoints rather than copying the TV session numbering. Suggested dependency order:

1. Greetings, self-introduction and short practical dialogues; bring existing numbers/family vocabulary into sentences.
2. Akkusativ objects and pronouns (mich/dich/ihn), then Dativ (mir/dir/ihm); teach article forms through concrete sentences.
3. Perfekt with haben, then sein and frequent irregular participles; contrast today/yesterday in personal stories.
4. Frequent prepositions with their case, possessives and everyday requests.
5. More modal verbs, man and imperative; integrate food, shopping and directions dialogues.
6. Separable and inseparable prefixes; connect present word order with Perfekt.
7. dass/weil clauses and deshalb main clauses.
8. Predicative adjectives, comparison, then attributive endings in small case-specific groups.
9. Cumulative practical tasks: introduce oneself, recount yesterday, explain wishes, arrange a meeting and ask directions.

Vocabulary expansion remains necessary. The current 282 entries are far short of the planned 2–3k, and vocabulary should enter contextual exercises and reviews rather than becoming an isolated word list. This is a roadmap, not a claim of completed A1/A2 coverage or measured superiority of either teaching method.
