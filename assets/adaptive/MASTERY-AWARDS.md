# Island mentor awards

Replaces the former eight story-clue fragments. The old clue text and flight decorations have been retired. This is optional recognition of perfect lesson tests; it does not gate lessons, the compass final or departure.

- An ordinary lesson seal still requires at least 80%.
- Each of the nine first-island lesson tests, including ship greetings, has one named mastery award and an issuing mentor. A test must score 100%; wrong and skipped answers prevent that score.
- Training, microphone repetition and correcting mistakes after a frozen test never issue an award. A new test is needed to improve the score.
- Ownership is derived from existing best scores: 100 for land lessons, 8/8 legacy-scaled greetings. Existing perfect results count immediately. No migration, new award storage, extra grades or invented award dates.
- Only the first perfect result produces `data-mastery-new="true"`. Repeating 100% or failing a later test preserves ownership without another celebration. Existing profiles remain isolated by their original keys.
- All nine awards grant the Island master title. The final compass and its own 100% explorer seal remain independent.

Mentors have explicit learning roles and matching navigation:

| Mentor | Displayed lessons | Teaching responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fox | 1, 2, final | Greetings, identifying the team, final challenge and departure |
| Nika | 4, 6 | Talking about yourself/your partner, coordinating we/you all |
| Tim | 5, 8 | Observing he/she and communicating group actions |
| Bruno | 3, 7, 9 | Action verbs, who lives where, who works |

Each roster card states its responsibility, lesson numbers and earned award count. Its button chooses an unpassed assigned lesson, then an unmastered assigned lesson, then a completed lesson for review. Fox includes the ship lesson. Award cards link directly to their own lesson introductions.

## Theory restoration

All original theory/rules, lesson goals, mode instructions, grading explanations and mistake recaps remain complete and openly visible. First-island grammar rules have a labelled Theory section. Greeting rules retain their exact text in a full-width Theory section inside the greeting encounter. Only story summaries and game collections keep the compact layout. No lesson content, recorded speech or assessment schedule changes.

## Artwork and motion

Existing approved portraits and their previously checked compact crops are reused. Stars are standard UI status symbols. No new character illustration, synthetic teaching audio or pronunciation measurement is introduced. Fresh awards have one subtle emblem animation and reuse the previous collection sound. Motion preferences and voice/microphone priority remain effective. No clue flight or new map particle animation remains.

Browser checks cover passing versus perfection, frozen corrections, ship and all eight land awards, retained old results, duplicate prevention, nine-award title, roster routing, visible complete theory, reload, profiles, English, reduced motion and 320–1280px.
