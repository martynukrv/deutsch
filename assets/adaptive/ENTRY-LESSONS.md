# Greetings and courtesy

The ship greeting lesson and Lina’s harbour lesson now use the common island
lesson cycle documented in LESSON-CYCLE.md: 30 training tasks through five
ordered stages, bounded correction, then a separate randomized 18-task test.

Original six phrases, artwork, whole recordings, Bitte’s two meanings and
canonical review bindings remain. Existing grade keys are unchanged:
deutsch16_greetings_prologue_v1 and deutsch16_ship_courtesy_v1. Their legacy
best/last fields stay normalized to the old out-of-eight scale for existing
route/star readers; lastCorrect, assessmentCount and lastPercent describe the
new test explicitly. Earned passes and highest best results are retained.
Training does not change these grade keys. Camp remains an ungraded two-item
review. No earlier advanced vocabulary is added.
